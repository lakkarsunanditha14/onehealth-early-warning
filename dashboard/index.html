How to read this dashboard

Each district is coloured by its risk score — the model's estimate of the chance it reports an outbreak in the next four weeks. Darker means higher.

The score is relative, not a promise. 30% does not mean an outbreak is 30% certain; it means this district ranks among the riskier ones right now. What matters is the ranking: act on the top of the list first.

Risk rises in the monsoon (shaded on the timeline). July outbreak rates run about six times January's, so a quiet winter week is normal, not a broken model.

Red outlines are outbreaks that actually happened — including ones the model rated low. Those misses are left visible on purpose.

What to do with it: work down "districts to watch" from the top. For each, the line underneath says why it is there — outbreaks nearby, recent cases locally, or just the season. Districts with nearby activity are worth a call to the block health officer; the rest are worth watching. Download the week's full ranking as CSV to circulate.
